# Darb (دَرب)

**Saudi Startup Feasibility & Risk Intelligence Platform**
King Saud University — College of Computer and Information Sciences
IT496 Graduation Project (GP1/GP2), 2026 — Group 17

## Introduction

Aspiring Saudi founders make market-entry decisions with information scattered across many sources. Darb's goal is to bring this together in one place: founders describe their idea in a conversation, and the platform assesses its feasibility, produces a Founder Intelligence Score (FIS), surfaces similar startups and market insights, and generates a personalized regulatory roadmap for launching in Saudi Arabia.

**Target sectors:** AI & Software, Food & Beverages, Fashion, Furniture.
